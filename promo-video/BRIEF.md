## Intent

A 60-second vertical promo for Meal Tracker (the user's own Android + Galaxy Watch app), told as an over-the-top
3 AM TV-shop infomercial: "Are YOU tired of not knowing when your khichdi leaves your stomach?!" Fast pace, cool,
satirical and creative; explain each feature quickly. User's words: "explain each feature in fast pace but cool
way with satire and creativity".

## Assets

- assets/screens/*.png — real screenshots captured from the user's S23 Ultra (720x1544) of every feature.

## Customizations

- No voiceover: punchy on-screen captions carry the satire; music + SFX.
- Music: synthesized locally with ffmpeg (HeyGen not signed in, MusicGen deps missing) — marked as host audio.
- "BUT WAIT, THERE'S MORE!" interstitials, starburst price tags, fake testimonials, "operators are standing by" ending.

## Notes

- Features to cover: photo → Gemini meal analysis, Samsung Health sync, energy battery, 48 h fuel & digestion
  timeline with forecast, muscle body map, digestion/chai-iron pairing, seasonal Gujarat fruit + Rajkot mandi
  prices, water, smart alarm on the Galaxy Watch, connections check.
- Satire stays affectionate (the user is the hero); no real brands mocked; app is free, not for sale.
